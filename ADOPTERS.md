# Adopters

This page lists organizations using Frames in production or actively evaluating it. If your team is shipping with Frames — whether you're rendering hundreds of videos a day, building agent-driven composition tooling, or experimenting with HTML-as-video for the first time — we'd love to hear about it.

Adding your organization helps the community understand how Frames is being used in the wild and makes it easier for new users to find peers solving similar problems.

## How to add your organization

Send a patch that adds a row to the table below. Keep entries short:

- **Organization** — your company or project name, linked to your website.
- **Contact** — a handle or contact person who can answer questions about your usage.
- **How Frames is used** — one sentence on the use case (e.g., "Personalized video at scale," "Agent-authored marketing assets," "Slides-to-video pipeline").

If you'd rather not be listed publicly, that's fine — mail <dev@hanzo.ai> instead. We always like hearing about how the project is being used.
